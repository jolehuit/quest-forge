# Quest Forge - Video Presentation Script

> Two presenters (A and B) alternate lines. Delivered in English.
> Stage directions in *italics*.

---

## PART 1 — THE HOOK (15-20s)

*Dark screen. Cinematic music fades in.*

**A:** Have you ever dreamed of creating your own video game...

**B:** ...with your own characters, your own story, your own world?

**A:** What if I told you that all you need... is a single conversation?

**B:** No code. No engine. No design skills.

**A:** Just your imagination.

*Beat. Title card appears: "QUEST FORGE"*

---

## PART 2 — THE CONCEPT (20-25s)

*Screen shows ChatGPT interface, ready to chat.*

**B:** Quest Forge is an app built for ChatGPT that turns any idea into a fully playable visual novel.

**A:** You describe your story — the setting, the characters, the vibe — and the AI builds the entire game for you.

**B:** We're talking AI-generated artwork, original music, voice narration...

**A:** ...puzzles, dialogue trees, a trust system with NPCs who actually react to what you say.

**B:** Every single game is unique. Every single game is yours.

---

## PART 3 — THE PHOTO MOMENT (15-20s)

*Both presenters face the camera together.*

**A:** But here's the twist. You don't just play a character...

**B:** ...you ARE the character.

*A takes a selfie of both of them with their phone. The photo appears on screen.*

**A:** We just took this photo. Now watch what happens.

*Time-lapse: the photo gets fed into the app. Fal AI generates a stylized portrait from it — dark fantasy oil painting style.*

**B:** That's us. In the game. As the main characters.

---

## PART 4 — LIVE DEMO TEASE (25-30s)

*Screen shows the Quest Forge title screen with their AI-generated portraits.*

**A:** So let's try it. We asked for a dark fantasy mystery in a haunted castle.

**B:** Two rogues investigating a cursed artifact. Six scenes. Two puzzles. One truth to uncover.

*Quick cuts of the gameplay: narration appearing, NPC dialogue, a puzzle screen, trust bar going up.*

**A:** The NPC talks to you in character. You can ask anything. Try to earn their trust... or confront them.

**B:** And when you hit a puzzle, you actually need to talk to the NPC to get hints. No cheating.

**A:** Every choice you make shapes what happens next. Different paths, different endings.

*Show the scene transition animation — fade out, new background fades in.*

**B:** And every scene comes with a brand new AI-generated background and soundtrack.

---

## PART 5 — THE CLOSE (10-15s)

*Both presenters back on camera.*

**A:** This is Quest Forge.

**B:** Your story. Your game. One conversation away.

*Both look at camera.*

**A:** Want to try it?

*End card: "Quest Forge — Built with Skybridge" + link/QR code.*

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Total runtime target:** ~90 seconds
- **Tone:** Confident, slightly mysterious, fun between the two presenters
- **Music:** Use a cinematic/fantasy ambient track, fade under dialogue
- **Key visual moments:**
  1. The selfie being taken (real) then transformed (AI portrait)
  2. The title screen with their generated portraits
  3. Quick gameplay montage (narration, NPC chat, puzzle, trust bar)
  4. Scene transition animation
- **Photo trick:** Take the selfie on camera, then cut to the AI-stylized version appearing as the character portrait in the game. This is the "wow" moment.
